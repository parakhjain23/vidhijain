# How to change the website

You do not need to know anything about code or websites to do this.
You ask an AI assistant to make the change, and it does the work.

Your website: **https://vidhijain.embarko.app**

## What you need, once

1. **Claude** (claude.ai) or **ChatGPT** (chatgpt.com) — either is fine.
2. **The project files.** Parakh will give you either a folder on your
   computer or a link to it at
   https://github.com/parakhjain23/visdhijain
3. **The Embarko key.** A long line of text starting `emb_`. This is a
   password for your website. Keep it somewhere safe and private. Anyone
   with it can change your site.

## Making a change

Open the AI, point it at the project folder, and say what you want in
ordinary words. You do not need to know which file anything is in — the
project has a guide inside it that tells the AI where to look.

Good examples to copy:

> Change the phone number on the website to +91 98765 43210, then deploy it.

> The Saturday hours are wrong. We close at 1pm now, not 2pm. Update and publish.

> Replace the three patient reviews with these real ones: [paste them]

> Add a sixth service called Post-natal rehabilitation, 45 minutes, treating
> the lower back and pelvis.

> Change the main headline to "Physiotherapy that gets you moving again".

Always end with **"then deploy it"** or **"and publish it live"**, otherwise
the AI may change the files without putting the change on the internet.

When it asks for the Embarko key, paste it in.

## Checking it worked

The AI should tell you the site is **live**. Wait for that word — a deploy
takes two to three minutes.

Then open https://vidhijain.embarko.app yourself and look. If you do not see
the change, refresh the page. If it is still wrong, tell the AI: *"I still
see the old text on the live site"* and let it sort it out.

## Things to be careful about

**Ask it to check before publishing.** A good habit is to say:
*"make the change, confirm the site still builds, then deploy"*.

**Changing the web address.** The address can only be changed once a week,
and the old one stops working immediately — anyone who saved the old link
will get an error. Do not change it casually.

**The booking form does not work yet.** Patients can choose a time and press
the button, but nothing is sent to you. **No booking you see on that form is
real, because none of them reach anybody.** Making it work properly is a
separate piece of work — ask Parakh.

**The details on the site are made up.** The patient reviews, the address,
the phone number and the "1,400+ patients" figure were written as examples
to show the design. They are not real and are currently visible to the
public under Dr. Jain's name. These should be replaced before you share the
website with anyone.

## If something goes wrong

Tell the AI what you see, in plain words — *"the website is showing an
error"* or *"the page is blank"*. It can look at the project and fix it.

Every past version is saved, so nothing is ever permanently lost. If you are
stuck, Parakh can restore the site to how it was.
